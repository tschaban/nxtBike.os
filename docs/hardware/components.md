## Purchasing notes

- Verify that the DALY unit is the 20S Li-ion version and that its RS485 port works with the supplied USB adapter.
- The hardware receives an existing, fully protected 5 V / 3 A supply. The 84 V to 12 V and 12 V to 5 V converters are outside this BOM.
- The Nextion NX4827P043-011C UART operates at 3.3 V and connects directly to the ESP32 UART; this was confirmed on the actual display.
- Confirm that a Hall sensor accepts 3.3 V or 5 V. Do not connect an unknown controller speed wire directly to the ESP32.
- Confirm the PAS mounting side, crank clearance, supply voltage and forward direction before purchase.
- Confirm the installed Sabvoton harness labels before connecting `LOW SPEED`, `HIGH SPEED` or `LOW BRAKE`; wire colors differ between revisions.
- Cheap DS18B20 probes may contain counterfeit sensors; test both probes before sealing the enclosure.
- The final manufacturing BOM will also contain exact PCB passives, connectors and protection parts after the schematic is completed.
